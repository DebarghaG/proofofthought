CLINICAL DECISION PROCEDURE
============================================================

Each diagnosis is a biconditional: the diagnosis holds if and only
if the required combination of radiological findings is met.

  Pneumonia (pneumonia_dx)
    Criteria: Or(focal_opacity, ground_glass_opacity, dense_opacity) and air_bronchogram

  Pneumothorax (pneumothorax_dx)
    Criteria: visible_pleural_line or (absent_lung_markings and deep_sulcus_sign)

  Pulmonary Edema (pulmonary_edema_dx)
    Criteria: (bilateral_infiltrates or bilateral_opacities) and (cephalization or kerley_b_lines or peribronchial_cuffing)

  Consolidation (consolidation_dx)
    Criteria: dense_opacity and (air_bronchogram or silhouette_sign_loss)

  Atelectasis (atelectasis_dx)
    Criteria: (volume_loss or fissure_displacement) and (linear_opacity or increased_opacity)

  Pleural Effusion (pleural_effusion_dx)
    Criteria: costophrenic_blunting or meniscus_sign or layering_fluid

  Fracture (fracture_dx)
    Criteria: cortical_break

  Cardiomegaly (cardiomegaly_dx)
    Criteria: enlarged_cardiac_silhouette or increased_cardiothoracic_ratio

  Emphysema (emphysema_dx)
    Criteria: hyperinflation and (flattened_diaphragm or bullae)

  Fibrosis (fibrosis_dx)
    Criteria: (reticular_opacities or honeycombing) and (traction_bronchiectasis or volume_loss)
